# Anmool Dairy Design System

## Brand Personality
Anmool Dairy ("Pure Milk, Pure Trust") is a farm-fresh dairy brand. The logo
signals **trust, cleanliness, and nature**: a navy cow silhouette, a green
leaf, a bright sky-blue circular border, and a milk-splash motif — all on
white. The design should feel clean, trustworthy, and fresh: closer to a
clinical-clean dairy/health brand than a rustic farmhouse, but still warm and
natural through the green leaf and organic milk-splash shapes.

Layout structure and component patterns (hero, alternating story sections,
product carousel, FAQ accordion, arched/circular image crops, floating CTAs)
follow the same proven pattern used for farm-to-table dairy sites — only the
**color palette, iconography, and type color roles** below are specific to
Anmool Dairy and should be used instead.

## Color Palette (extracted from the logo)

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Navy Blue | `#002060` | Logo wordmark, headings, cow icon, nav bar, primary text on light backgrounds, primary buttons |
| Secondary / Accent | Fresh Green | `#5C9A2E` | "DAIRY" wordmark, leaf icon, secondary headings, success/organic call-outs, divider lines |
| Tertiary / Highlight | Sky Blue | `#0E7FC0` | Circular border, links, icon accents, secondary buttons, badges |
| Highlight — light | Pale Sky Blue | `#8FC7E8` | Water-splash gradient, soft section backgrounds, hover states |
| Background | White | `#FFFFFF` | Main page background |
| Body text | Deep Navy / Charcoal | `#0F1A33` | Paragraph copy (slightly softened from pure navy for readability at body size) |

**Rules:**
- Navy is the dominant brand color for text, headings, and primary UI
  elements (nav, buttons) — it replaces black as the "ink" color.
- Green is the accent for anything organic/trust-related: "Pure", "Fresh",
  "Organic", checkmarks, leaf iconography, section eyebrow labels.
- Sky blue is used for the encircling border motif, secondary buttons, links,
  and any "purity/water/milk" visual cues (droplets, waves, splashes).
- Keep backgrounds white or very pale blue — avoid heavy color blocking;
  let navy/green/sky-blue do the work as accents against white, mirroring
  the logo's white base.
- Do not introduce black as a primary color — navy takes that role
  throughout (nav bar, footer, button fills).

## Typography

- **Logo / wordmark**: bold serif for "ANMOOL" in navy, bold sans/slab for
  "DAIRY" in green flanked by thin horizontal rule lines, with a small navy
  cow-head icon + green leaf above. Tagline "PURE MILK, PURE TRUST" in navy,
  small caps, letter-spaced sans-serif.
- **Hero / large headings**: bold serif or slab serif in navy (or white when
  over a photo), large scale, confident and clean.
- **Section eyebrows**: small sans-serif line in green or sky blue, sits
  above the bold headline as a lead-in (mirrors the leaf/organic cue).
- **Section headline**: bold, uppercase, navy or green depending on emphasis
  — use green when the message is about purity/freshness/organic, navy for
  general section titles.
- **Body copy**: clean sans-serif, deep navy/charcoal, generous line-height.
- **Buttons/labels**: sans-serif, medium-bold, uppercase or title case.

**Signature heading pattern** (recolored version of the layout pattern):
```
[small sans-serif eyebrow in sky blue or green]
[BOLD UPPERCASE HEADLINE — navy or green]
        🐄🌿  (thin navy/green rule — small cow or leaf icon — thin rule)
[body paragraph, deep navy/charcoal]
```

## Iconography & Decorative Elements

- Reuse the logo's own motifs as recurring UI icons: a fine-line **cow head
  silhouette** (navy) and a **leaf** (green) — use one or both as small
  dividers under section headlines, echoing the logo mark.
- A **water-splash / droplet motif** (sky blue, gradient from mid to pale
  blue) can be used as a decorative background element at the bottom of
  hero sections or banners — directly lifted from the logo's splash base.
- Icons should stay simple, fine-line, single or dual-color (navy + green,
  or navy + sky blue) — no flat multi-color illustration sets.
- A circular badge/border treatment (sky-blue ring on white) works well for
  trust badges, certifications, or profile-style imagery, echoing the logo's
  circular frame.

## Layout Patterns
*(structure carried over — only recolor per palette above)*

- **Announcement bar**: navy background, white/pale-blue text.
- **Nav bar**: white or navy background; logo left, utility icons right in
  navy or white depending on bar color; sky-blue used for hover/active states.
- **Hero**: full-bleed farm/product photography, navy overlay for text
  legibility if needed, white or navy headline depending on contrast.
- **Alternating storytelling sections**: image/text sections alternating
  sides scroll to scroll, headline in navy/green per the signature pattern.
- **Product carousel**: products in pale-sky-blue or white organic
  arch/oval shapes, navy "Add"/"Buy" pill buttons, sky-blue arrow nav.
- **Flat-lay product hero**: full range photographed together on white or
  pale-blue background.
- **FAQ**: accordion cards, navy "+" icon circles, white cards, sky-blue
  hover accents.
- **Floating CTAs**: WhatsApp bubble (standard green `#25D366`, bottom-right)
  and a navy or sky-blue "Inquire Now" / "Order Now" pill (bottom-left).

## Image Treatment

- Clean, bright, natural-light photography — cows, farm scenes, product
  bottles, pouring milk / droplet close-ups (ties to the splash motif).
- Arched-top and full-circle crops (circular crop pairs especially well with
  the logo's own circular frame) to break the rectangular grid.
- Product shots on white or pale sky-blue background, true-to-life colors.

## Buttons & CTAs

- Primary buttons: solid navy background, white uppercase bold text,
  pill or rounded rectangle.
- Secondary buttons: sky-blue outline or fill, navy or white text.
- Use green sparingly for buttons — reserve it for "organic/eco" specific
  call-outs (e.g. "100% Organic") rather than general CTAs.

## Voice & Copy Tone

- Clean, confident, trust-forward — lean on words like "pure," "trust,"
  "fresh," "natural" (directly echoing the tagline "Pure Milk, Pure Trust").
- Short, reassuring declarative sentences; avoid corporate jargon.

## Quick Checklist for New Pages/Components
- [ ] Palette limited to navy (`#002060`), green (`#5C9A2E`), sky blue
      (`#0E7FC0` / `#8FC7E8`), and white
- [ ] Navy used as the primary "ink"/UI color instead of black
- [ ] Green reserved for purity/organic/freshness emphasis
- [ ] Sky blue used for borders, links, secondary buttons, water/splash cues
- [ ] Cow-head and/or leaf icon divider under section headlines
- [ ] At least one arched or circular image crop if imagery is present
- [ ] Alternating image/text layout for multi-section story flow
- [ ] Buttons: solid navy primary, sky-blue secondary, white text
- [ ] Floating WhatsApp (green) + navy/sky-blue "Order Now" CTA if a full page
